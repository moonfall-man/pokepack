## Concept

Verdant Wilds is a monster-taming RPG with the bones of a Game-Boy-era classic — tile-grid overworld, tall grass, turn-based battles — fused with survival crafting: everything you fight with, you first gathered and built with your own hands. This sprint that promise finally reaches the grass: pulled fiber becomes a snare at the craft menu, a campfire cooks your berries, fences rise on the grid, and Thistlet and Wickerbill wake in the tall grass to be met, fought, and fled — each on its own map, with the catch itself arriving as its own ticket at a future table.

## Design pillars

1. **The grid is the truth.** Placement and encounters land on the same tiles movement does: a placed fence blocks exactly like a map fence, a tall-grass step rolls the seeded dice, and any tile still answers "can I walk it, gather it, build on it?" at a glance.
2. **Survival feeds taming — the battle is the price mechanism.** Snares are fiber-priced, wild hits press hunger (survival is the health bar), and cooking becomes the battle-prep move — Marcus's catch economics stand ready for the beat that lands next sprint. This sprint the thread honestly ends at flee, and every document says so.
3. **Original and readable, silhouette-first.** Both species enter per the posted sheet: opposite silhouette classes readable in grayscale at 1x, an ink-beat reveal doing the work audio can't, and the red family keeps exactly two members — zero red pixels on either creature. The day-of shipped-scale audit runs anyway; it is acceptance, not advice.
